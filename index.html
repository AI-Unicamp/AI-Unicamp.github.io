Artificial Intelligence through Multimodal Signal Processing.

We study the relationship between signals, perception and intelligence: how multimodal signals shape intelligent systems, and how artificial intelligence can help us understand, process and generate them.

About

Research at the intersection of AI and multimodal perception.

AIMS is a research group at the University of Campinas (Unicamp). Our work connects artificial intelligence and signal processing, with multimodal perception as a central scientific perspective.

Research

From signals to perception, learning and intelligent systems.

Research outputs will bring together papers, theses and dissertations, software, artifacts and datasets in a single, durable catalogue.

People

A research collective built across generations.

The group brings together faculty, senior researchers, postdoctoral, graduate and undergraduate researchers, with alumni remaining part of the laboratory's history.
